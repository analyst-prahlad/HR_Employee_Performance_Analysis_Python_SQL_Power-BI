# HR Employee Performance Analysis

## Overview
The HR Employee Analytics Dashboard project focuses on analyzing employee data to uncover workforce trends, employee attrition patterns, salary insights, and departmental performance. The objective is to support HR decision-making through data-driven insights and interactive visualizations.

## Business Problem
Employee attrition and workforce management are critical challenges for organizations. This project aims to identify factors influencing employee turnover, understand workforce demographics, and provide actionable insights to improve employee retention and organizational performance.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze employee attrition trends
- Evaluate department-wise workforce distribution
- Study the impact of overtime on attrition
- Analyze salary patterns across departments
- Explore employee demographics and performance
- Build an interactive HR analytics dashboard

- Job Role
- Monthly Income
- Attrition Status

## Key Insights
- Research & Development department recorded the highest attrition.
- Employees working overtime showed a higher likelihood of leaving.
- Significant salary differences exist across departments.
- Workforce concentration is higher in specific job roles.
- Overall attrition rate is approximately 16%.
